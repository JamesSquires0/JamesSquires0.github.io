ZFC Axioms

James Squires • Feb 2, 2026

I just read the ZFC axioms. To be honest, it was a bit anticlimactic! I think this is because I already intuited most of my gaps; it's still nice to formalize the ZFC axioms though. To me this just begs the question of a model; I'm interested in looking at model theory now. I found analyzing ZFC axioms through the sense of constructivism to be quite interesting. It seems the power set axiom and the choice axiom are highly non-constructive in the sense that we can't compute many of the sets given.

Concomitant with the ZFC axioms is Peano arithmetic. This is where removing LEM would really crash the party. As you may or may not know, intuitionistic logic defenstrates the law of trichotomy amongst other things.

I recommend this read: https://math.uchicago.edu/~may/REU2016/REUPapers/Wilson.pdf
