Thinking is Outsourced to the Environment

James Squires • Feb 12, 2026

Memes are distributed. They are embedded on the neuronal level, but also on the environmental level. This is because your behavior causes interaction between the environment and you. The environment prompts our behavior. Thus our environment has influenced our neurology. The environment has decided what combination of thinking to access; it has shaped the neurology of a person. Inductively, we can see the environment recursively embeds itself in the neurology of a person (and vice versa), therefore the information landscape that our memes live on is constituted both by the environment and mind.
