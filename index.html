Bio

I'm an honors mathematics and physics major at UGA. I currently have aspirations of building safer and more powerful AI. Ultimately I'd want to see a world where AI can legitimately perform reasoning and complex tasks alongside humans. The computational ability of computers would enable development of theories in otherwise intractable complex systems, opening up paths that we never thought possible.

I currently do research at UGA in:

  • Machine learning + persistent homology for physics education
  • Non-LTE radiative transfer modeling + best-fit in protoplanetary disks using Radex
  • Categorical semantics of QDMs

I intend to do a PhD in formal verification. I'm interested in investigating AI interpretability and safety through a mathematical lens. I naively suspect that homotopy type theory is a promising lead here.
